Innovation

Vaxogene is building research capabilities at the intersection of organoid biology, genomics, bioinformatics, molecular biology, and translational science.

Focus: Capabilities in Development

Our current focus is on developing and evaluating practical research approaches that connect experimental models with molecular and computational analysis. These capabilities are being expanded through internal development, pilot work, and scientific collaborations.

Organoids & 3D Models

Building capabilities in biologically relevant 3D models for disease research, mechanistic studies, and therapeutic evaluation.

Genomics & Bioinformatics

Applying genomic, transcriptomic, and computational approaches to study disease biology, pathways, biomarkers, and biological variation.

Translational Research

Connecting computational findings with molecular, cellular, organoid, and preclinical research approaches to support further biological validation.

Innovation in Development

Vaxogene is continuing to define and develop its research programs. New capabilities, platforms, and collaborative projects will be added as they are established and supported by appropriate scientific validation.
